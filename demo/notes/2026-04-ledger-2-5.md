# Ledger 2.5

Released 2026-04-03.

- Multi-currency accounts. A ledger can hold balances in more than one currency, and the statement
  shows each one separately rather than converting on the way out.
- The audit export gained a `settledAt` column. Older exports leave it empty.
- Removed the `/v1/accounts/:id/summary` endpoint that 2.3 deprecated. Use `/v1/accounts/:id`.

Run `ledger migrate 2.5` before starting the service. The migration is online and takes about a
minute per million rows.
