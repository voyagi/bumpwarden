# Ledger 2.4

Released 2026-02-11.

- Statement exports now stream, so a year of rows no longer holds the whole file in memory.
- The reconciliation job retries a failed batch three times before it parks the batch for review.
- Fixed: a payment posted at exactly midnight UTC landed in the previous day's statement.

Upgrading from 2.3 needs no migration. The export endpoint keeps its old shape.
