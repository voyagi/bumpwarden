# Ledger 2.6

Released 2026-07-22.

- Scheduled transfers. A transfer can carry a start date and an interval, and the scheduler holds it
  until the account has the balance to cover it.
- Statement PDFs render in the account's own locale instead of the operator's.
- Fixed: the reconciliation job counted a parked batch as settled in the daily total.

No migration. Scheduled transfers are off until an operator enables them per account.
